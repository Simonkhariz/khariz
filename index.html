Questions?

The answers you’re looking for before bringing your project to life.

How do we get started?

Choose the package that best fits your needs and submit the inquiry form. I’ll review your goals and requirements, then confirm the scope, timeline and final price before we begin.

Why don’t you do calls?

I am available for 1-on-1 strategy calls and direct communication via WhatsApp, Phone, Slack, email or Discord to keep us aligned and focused on your goals.

What if I already have a finished design?

That’s exactly what the Development package is for. I turn finished designs from Figma or other tools into responsive, pixel-perfect web applications using React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS.

How many pages are included?

Development and Landing Page include one page; extras are quoted separately. Full Website includes a landing page plus up to four subpages. Landing Page and Full Website include a free 404 page.

How does payment work?

Development and Landing Page are paid 50% before work begins and 50% before handover. Larger Full Website projects can be split into milestones.

How is the final website delivered?

After approval and final payment, I transfer full source code via GitHub repository, production build files, and deployment instructions to your workspace. You then own 100% of the codebase, domain, and infrastructure.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are available depending on project progress: full refunds are provided if work has not yet started, and partial refunds are offered if only part of the service has been delivered. Clear communication keeps us aligned.
